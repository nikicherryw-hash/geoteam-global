Scope

A structured approach to aviation procurement

GeoTeam Global reviews enquiries on a case-by-case basis. The content below describes product areas and identification requirements, not an unconditional supply or service guarantee.

Procurement teams can submit a single Part Number or a multi-line BOM. We ask for the manufacturer, requested condition, acceptable documentation, quantity and destination so each supplier response can be assessed on comparable terms.

Availability, acceptable records, delivery lead time and export permission vary by item and seller. We do not represent ourselves as an aircraft operator, airworthiness authority or authorized distributor of every brand listed.

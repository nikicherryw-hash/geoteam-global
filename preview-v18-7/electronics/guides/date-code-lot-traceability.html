Date Codes, Lot Codes & Traceability

Specify component age, mixed-lot permissions and traceability without overclaiming stock conditions.

All guides ↗

Identify product marking conventions

Different manufacturers use different date and assembly-code conventions. Compare codes to official information for the exact series.

Agree age limits explicitly

A blanket maximum date-code age is not appropriate for every device. Define contractual requirements based on the application and manufacturer storage guidance.

Request lot segregation

Determine whether mixed lots are acceptable and how each delivered package will be identified on invoices and labels.

Record chain of custody

Ask for evidence connecting the supplied lot to its claimed original source if traceability is a purchase requirement.

Check storage conditions

Moisture sensitivity, oxidation risk and solderability may depend more on storage than calendar age alone.

Review deviations before approval

Missing provenance or different markings should trigger technical and procurement review, not undocumented acceptance.

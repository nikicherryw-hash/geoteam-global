Capacitor Substitution and Selection

Specify dielectric, voltage, size, bias and lifetime, not just capacitance.

All guides ↗

Record the original specification

List capacitance, dielectric, case size, tolerance, voltage, temperature and required termination style.

Evaluate DC bias

MLCC effective capacitance can vary with applied DC bias and temperature; compare characteristic curves where needed.

Consider ESR and ripple

Electrolytic and film technologies differ in ESR, ripple-current capability, expected life and failure behavior.

Check mechanical fit

Height, pad geometry and soldering profile can prevent replacement even when electrical values are similar.

Confirm application constraints

Critical power, timing or safety-related circuits may have restrictions against generic substitution.

Document approvals

Obtain explicit design-owner sign-off for a different dielectric, manufacturer series or package.

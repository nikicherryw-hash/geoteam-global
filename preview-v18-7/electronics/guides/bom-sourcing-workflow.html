How to Prepare a BOM for Component Sourcing

Avoid ambiguous line items and false substitutions during multi-line electronics procurement.

All guides ↗

Keep one row per requested specification

For each BOM line capture internal reference, manufacturer, full MPN, quantity per assembly, build quantity, package and approved alternates.

Preserve manufacturer names

Avoid using reseller SKUs as if they were manufacturer part numbers. If a distributor SKU is provided, separately resolve its actual manufacturer ordering code.

Normalize units and quantities

Separate pieces, reels, cut tape and trays. Preserve packaging multiples and per-line minimum order constraints.

Mark lifecycle and criticality

Flag components that are NRND, obsolete, allocation-sensitive, safety-related or impossible to substitute without redesign.

Demand line-by-line offers

Return proposed manufacturer, quoted MPN, condition, lot/date code rules, quantity available, price, lead time, and deviation notes on every BOM line.

Review with engineering

An alternative BOM is not validated merely because a vendor recommends it. Obtain design-owner sign-off for substitutions and qualification work.

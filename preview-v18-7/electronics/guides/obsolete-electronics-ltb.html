Electronics / Technical buying guide

Obsolete Components and Last-Time-Buy Planning

Structure EOL sourcing without treating a suggested alternate as drop-in equivalent.

All guides ↗

Verify lifecycle status

Confirm the exact manufacturer ordering part and its published lifecycle or product-change information. Reseller labels may be stale.

Quantify lifetime demand

Estimate service quantities, production schedules, maintenance requirements and safety stock before considering a last-time buy.

Evaluate storage and shelf life

Define packaging, environmental storage and inspection controls suitable for the device over the planned period.

Separate authentic inventory from equivalents

Existing original inventory, refurbished or reclaimed components and new substitutes are materially different commercial offers.

Plan redesign with engineering

Qualification, firmware, pinout and layout changes require design-owner decisions, not sourcing assumptions.

Maintain decision records

Log supplier, proposed quantities, lifecycle evidence, test requirements, approval and any restricted-market screening.

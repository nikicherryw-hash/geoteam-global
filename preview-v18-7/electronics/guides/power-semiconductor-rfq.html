MOSFET, IGBT & Power-Device RFQ Checklist

Document switching, gate-drive and thermal constraints for high-power electronic devices.

All guides ↗

Begin with electrical ratings

Capture voltage class, continuous/pulsed current, on-state losses, gate threshold and the true switching envelope.

Check gate-drive compatibility

Device gate charge, resistance and drive voltage can significantly affect switching losses and EMI behavior.

Assess thermal assembly

Specify package, cooling, case temperature, mounting torque requirements and thermal interface material where applicable.

Compare dynamic parameters

Review switching and reverse recovery behavior, short-circuit withstand and safe-operating-area information where relevant.

Confirm qualification and source

Choose the required device grade, traceability, packing and manufacturer document version for the application.

Treat equivalents carefully

Power component alternates require engineering approval and potentially redesign or thermal testing.

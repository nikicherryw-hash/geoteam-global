Sensor Accuracy, Interface & Calibration

Specify usable sensor performance and evidence for the installed application.

All guides ↗

Capture the full measured range

A sensor listed with a broad nominal range may not meet the required accuracy at the actual operating point.

Clarify output and protocol

Document analog or digital output, voltage/current, timing and driver requirements.

Clarify environmental behavior

Temperature drift, vibration, humidity, package mounting and mechanical stress can change performance.

Specify calibration needs

State whether factory calibration, adjustment data or traceable calibration documentation is necessary.

Compare revisions and alternatives

Part-family revisions and calibration coefficients may change compatibility.

Preserve system approval

A replacement sensor must be approved against the actual machine and control-loop requirements.

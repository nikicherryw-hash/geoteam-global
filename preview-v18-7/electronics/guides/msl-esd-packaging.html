MSL, ESD and Electronics Packaging in RFQs

Specify component handling and packaging conditions that protect assembly quality.

All guides ↗

Determine moisture sensitivity

Ask whether moisture-sensitive devices require dry packing, desiccant, humidity indicator and manufacturer-specified labeling.

Differentiate package types

State tape-and-reel, cut tape, tray, tube or bulk format. Assembly equipment can require exact pocket and orientation options.

Preserve ESD controls

Specify approved ESD packaging and handling requirements where relevant; a normal plastic bag is not evidence of protective packing.

Request storage and shipping details

Shipping duration, humidity exposure and repacking can affect sensitive components and should be agreed with the supplier.

Set acceptance evidence

Decide which exterior labels, package seals and photographic records must accompany a shipment.

Route exceptions to quality

Broken seals, inconsistent labels or mismatched packaging need documented buyer review and should not be silently accepted.

Engineering context

Why technical identification matters

Wireless modules are not interchangeable solely because they support the same protocol or frequency band.

Confirm antenna type, RF matching, host interface, firmware variants and the regulatory region of the finished design.

RF supply requests should also identify whether the buyer needs an IC, certified module, evaluation kit or a configured radio assembly.

Equipment scope

Typical components and systems

  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth ICs
  • cellular modems
  • GNSS receivers
  • RF amplifiers
  • transceivers and RF filters

Required RFQ data

frequency bands, modulation, output power, interface, antenna options, revision, regional certification and deployment market.

Additionally include full model code, quantity, required documentation, delivery location and expected date. Technical approval is separate from sourcing.

Questions

Technical procurement FAQ

What information is needed to request an offer?

Start with frequency bands, modulation, output power, interface, antenna options, revision, regional certification and deployment market. Also specify quantity, condition, destination and acceptable documentation.

Can a similar model be used?

A suggested replacement must be evaluated against original design requirements, applicable standards and the machine or process owner’s approval. A listing or part number similarity alone is not evidence of equivalence.

Is this an inventory or authorized dealer catalog?

No. Brand names are identification references; availability and commercial terms are confirmed individually after a specific RFQ.

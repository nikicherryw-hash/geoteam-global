Engineering context

Why technical identification matters

Test instrument offers must name the exact model and installed option licenses; an instrument family name is not enough.

Check probe, cable, software, calibration and traceable test records before comparing supplier offers.

Used or refurbished instruments require explicit buyer acceptance, condition records and verification of supported firmware.

Equipment scope

Typical components and systems

  • oscilloscopes
  • spectrum analyzers
  • electronic loads
  • multimeters and precision meters
  • programmable power supplies

Required RFQ data

instrument model, channel count, bandwidth/resolution, accessory list, calibration requirements and mains voltage.

Additionally include full model code, quantity, required documentation, delivery location and expected date. Technical approval is separate from sourcing.

Questions

Technical procurement FAQ

What information is needed to request an offer?

Start with instrument model, channel count, bandwidth/resolution, accessory list, calibration requirements and mains voltage. Also specify quantity, condition, destination and acceptable documentation.

Can a similar model be used?

A suggested replacement must be evaluated against original design requirements, applicable standards and the machine or process owner’s approval. A listing or part number similarity alone is not evidence of equivalence.

Is this an inventory or authorized dealer catalog?

No. Brand names are identification references; availability and commercial terms are confirmed individually after a specific RFQ.

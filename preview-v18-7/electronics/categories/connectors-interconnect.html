Engineering context

Why technical identification matters

Connector ordering is incomplete without the mating pair, termination method and applicable contact or backshell part numbers.

Similar housings can have different keys, plating, environmental seals and current ratings.

Cable assembly substitution should check insertion life, shielding, impedance and installation clearance.

Equipment scope

Typical components and systems

  • board-to-board connectors
  • wire-to-board headers
  • circular connectors
  • RF connectors and coax
  • cable assemblies and contacts

Questions

Technical procurement FAQ

What information is needed to request an offer?

Start with mating half, contact count, pitch, gender, locking type, plating, cable gauge and exact series code. Also specify quantity, condition, destination and acceptable documentation.

Can a similar model be used?

A suggested replacement must be evaluated against original design requirements, applicable standards and the machine or process owner’s approval. A listing or part number similarity alone is not evidence of equivalence.

Is this an inventory or authorized dealer catalog?

No. Brand names are identification references; availability and commercial terms are confirmed individually after a specific RFQ.

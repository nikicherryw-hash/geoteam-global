Scope

Engineering-led enquiry preparation

Technical guidance for specifying marine, offshore, inland waterway and port equipment before requesting offers.

Marine sourcing depends on the installed vessel or facility, manufacturer, equipment model, serial number, engineering drawing, operating conditions and required certificates. This reference catalog covers four use environments: commercial ships, inland vessels, offshore units and port or shipyard infrastructure. It does not limit the global part-number search to those areas.

An equipment ecosystem reference does not indicate dealership, confirmed availability, on-site installation, vessel classification acceptance or a standing stockholding. Offers, certification scope, technical suitability and export conditions require individual confirmation.

Engineering context

Why technical identification matters

The gearbox nameplate must be matched to ratio and input/output rotation; a common housing may be configured for multiple reduction ratios.

Record coupling bolt circle, shaft diameter and installation envelope before proposing any replacement mechanical interface.

Stern-tube and shaft seal specifications depend on water exposure, lubrication system and class-approved arrangement.

Request service history and defect photographs for repair kits, because a wear-related failure can require associated bearing or lubrication components.

Equipment scope

Typical components and systems

  • reduction gears
  • marine clutches
  • flexible couplings
  • shaft seals
  • stern-tube bearings
  • lubrication and cooling accessories

Required RFQ data

gearbox model and serial, ratio, torque, shaft dimensions, coupling arrangement, oil grade, wear measurements.

Additionally include full model code, quantity, required documentation, delivery location and expected date. Technical approval is separate from sourcing.

Questions

Technical procurement FAQ

What information is needed to request an offer?

Start with gearbox model and serial, ratio, torque, shaft dimensions, coupling arrangement, oil grade, wear measurements. Also specify quantity, condition, destination and acceptable documentation.

Can a similar model be used?

A suggested replacement must be evaluated against original design requirements, applicable standards and the machine or process owner’s approval. A listing or part number similarity alone is not evidence of equivalence.

Is this an inventory or authorized dealer catalog?

No. Brand names are identification references; availability and commercial terms are confirmed individually after a specific RFQ.

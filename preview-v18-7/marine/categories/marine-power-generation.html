Engineering context

Why technical identification matters

Marine generation often relies on strict coordination between governor, AVR and switchboard protection; replacing one component can alter sharing and synchronization behavior.

Document operating frequency, environmental and vibration conditions, class scope and interface with existing protection.

Emergency generator requirements should be distinguished from hotel or auxiliary power; the required approval basis is not interchangeable.

For older machines, request existing wiring diagrams and excitation details rather than ordering by nominal kVA alone.

Questions

Technical procurement FAQ

What information is needed to request an offer?

Start with engine and alternator type, rated kVA, frequency, voltage, excitation, parallel operation and emergency duty classification. Also specify quantity, condition, destination and acceptable documentation.

Can a similar model be used?

A suggested replacement must be evaluated against original design requirements, applicable standards and the machine or process owner’s approval. A listing or part number similarity alone is not evidence of equivalence.

Is this an inventory or authorized dealer catalog?

No. Brand names are identification references; availability and commercial terms are confirmed individually after a specific RFQ.

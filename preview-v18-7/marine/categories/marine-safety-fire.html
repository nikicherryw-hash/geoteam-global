Engineering context

Why technical identification matters

Safety equipment should never be advertised as approved for installation merely because a nominal product family appears in a catalogue.

Specify vessel flag/class context and required certificate or service documentation for each item.

Some lifesaving appliances must be serviced by an approved station; purchasing replacement hardware does not substitute for regulatory servicing.

Fire-system changes may affect piping, discharge logic or interlocks and should be reviewed by a competent marine engineer.

Equipment scope

Questions

Technical procurement FAQ

What information is needed to request an offer?

Start with vessel context, certificate/type approval, model, serial, service history, inspection due date and installation requirements. Also specify quantity, condition, destination and acceptable documentation.

Can a similar model be used?

A suggested replacement must be evaluated against original design requirements, applicable standards and the machine or process owner’s approval. A listing or part number similarity alone is not evidence of equivalence.

Is this an inventory or authorized dealer catalog?

No. Brand names are identification references; availability and commercial terms are confirmed individually after a specific RFQ.

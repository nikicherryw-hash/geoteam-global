Engineering context

Why technical identification matters

Valve naming conventions differ among marine catalogues: DN, PN, JIS flange series and ANSI classes cannot be treated as interchangeable.

Provide valve drawing or face-to-face dimensions where installation space is limited, and confirm marine service, closure direction and actuator fail action.

For fire, cargo or safety-critical service, documentation and class acceptance need to be confirmed before any purchase commitment.

Marine piping often combines dissimilar metals; materials and galvanic corrosion controls should be reviewed by the vessel engineer.

Questions

Technical procurement FAQ

What information is needed to request an offer?

Start with nominal size, pressure class, body/trim material, medium, temperature, connection standard, actuation and certification scope. Also specify quantity, condition, destination and acceptable documentation.

Can a similar model be used?

A suggested replacement must be evaluated against original design requirements, applicable standards and the machine or process owner’s approval. A listing or part number similarity alone is not evidence of equivalence.

Is this an inventory or authorized dealer catalog?

No. Brand names are identification references; availability and commercial terms are confirmed individually after a specific RFQ.

Thruster and Propulsion Spare Parts Checklist

Collect the required data for propulsion, pitch control and azimuth equipment.

All guides ↗

Propulsion identity

Record thruster manufacturer, type, serial, propeller arrangement and available machinery drawings. Control and propulsion components may have different OEM labels.

Power and mechanics

Identify shaft speed, rating, gear ratio, rotation direction and coupling dimensions. Propeller geometry should come from approved drawings, not inferred from photos.

Hydraulic and control scope

For controllable-pitch systems, specify hydraulics, oil supply, feedback and control system type. Firmware and controller variants can affect compatibility.

Inspection decision

Vessel engineering and class requirements determine installation eligibility. A sourcing quotation is not a commissioning approval or propulsion-system design assessment.

Offshore Equipment and Hazardous-Area Data

Identify offshore components with pressure, corrosion and hazardous-area constraints.

All guides ↗

Site classification

Record zone/classification basis, gas group, temperature class and applicable equipment rating from the operator’s approved documentation.

Pressure and corrosion

State design depth or pressure, temperature, corrosion allowance, coating and material standard for subsea or deck exposure.

Evidence

Confirm that certificates correspond to the exact part and installed configuration; similar model names can carry different certifications.

Change control

Qualified engineers and relevant authorities must review modifications to process or electrical safety systems. Sourcing does not replace this approval.

Urgent Vessel Spare Parts and Port Delivery

Define an urgent marine RFQ without promising impossible port-call deadlines.

All guides ↗

Capture timing precisely

Record vessel ETA/ETD, port and terminal, required on-board timing, customs/agent contact and local cutoffs. Port arrival and actual ship delivery are not synonymous.

Confirm goods status

Do not equate an online listing with available stock. Confirm physical location, condition, packaging and written handover readiness.

Coordinate logistics

Check delivery address, local agent procedures, customs status, transport mode, dimensions and dangerous-goods details before confirming terms.

Avoid false certainty

Provide the customer with the confirmed supplier lead time and unresolved dependencies; do not guarantee ship attendance or delivery until all parties confirm.

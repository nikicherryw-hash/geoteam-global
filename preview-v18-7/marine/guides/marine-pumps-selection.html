Marine Pump RFQ: Flow, Head, Medium and Materials

Build an accurate seawater, ballast, bilge or firewater pump request.

All guides ↗

Hydraulic duty

Provide flow, differential head, suction conditions, NPSH where required, system curve, operating speed and pump orientation. The same nominal flange may be shared by pumps with different duty points.

Wet-end material

State seawater or freshwater chemistry, temperature, solids and existing impeller, casing and seal materials. A generic stainless steel designation is not sufficient evidence of corrosion suitability.

Motor and installation

Identify motor voltage, frequency, protection, coupling, mounting footprint and control arrangement. For self-priming or emergency systems, explain startup behavior.

Documentation

Request curves, assembly drawings and material/inspection certificates that are applicable to the transaction. Firewater or other essential systems may require vessel-specific approval.

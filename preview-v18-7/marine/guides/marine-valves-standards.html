Marine Valves: DN, PN, Flanges and Actuation

Compare marine valve offers without mixing incompatible pressure and connection conventions.

All guides ↗

Valve duty

Identify process medium, design pressure, temperature, flow direction, shutoff class and safety function. Salt water, fuel oil and steam may require different trim and seals.

Mechanical interface

Specify exact flange standard and drilling, face-to-face length, end connections and body material. DN and PN alone do not define the complete interface.

Actuator/control

Record manual, pneumatic, electric or hydraulic operation, power supply, fail-open/fail-closed behavior, feedback and stroke time.

Certification and comparison

Where vessel, flag or class rules demand evidence, request the relevant approvals and records. Written deviations are necessary before accepting different materials or functional behavior.

Export Control & Aviation Procurement: Responsible RFQ Preparation

Why end-user, end-use and destination checks belong in the initial buying process.

All buyer guides ↗

Screen the transaction

Aerospace goods, avionics, software and technical data can be subject to export-control or sanctions requirements. Classification and applicable rules depend on product, origin, parties, destination and use.

Provide accurate information

Do not invent or conceal a destination or customer when disclosure is required. Where facts are unavailable, stop the transaction and request legitimate clarification rather than substituting a fictional end user.

Differentiate logistics from origin

Country of manufacture, current stock location, seller country, shipment route and final destination are distinct fields; do not merge them in internal records.

Treat documents as controlled

Technical manuals, software, drawings and certificates may have their own redistribution or export restrictions; access should be lawful and role-appropriate.

Hold binding actions

Before ordering or shipping a potentially controlled component, obtain qualified regulatory review and the necessary permissions or licenses where required.

Purpose of this guide

This is a procurement workflow guide, not a legal determination. The responsible parties must verify current requirements in the applicable jurisdictions.

Aviation procurement guide

Buying Aviation Test & Ground Support Equipment

Technical and documentary requirements for instruments, tooling and GSE.

All buyer guides ↗

Specify the job, not only the brand

Request the specific maintenance task, technical procedure or measurement function together with the OEM model. General-purpose tools may not satisfy specialized aircraft requirements.

Define measurement performance

Record range, uncertainty, resolution, bandwidth or torque requirements as appropriate to the instrument. Note relevant accessories, options and connectors.

Clarify calibration status

State whether calibration is required at delivery, acceptable laboratory standards, traceability requirements and maximum allowed age of the certificate.

Check electrical and regional fit

Confirm voltage, plug standard, software/licensing options and transport constraints. Identical model names may represent different option configurations.

Separate equipment from service

Some tools need recurring service, specialized adapters or software support. Include these in the commercial comparison where material.

Require operator confirmation

The maintenance organization should independently confirm that a proposed tool meets the applicable procedure and quality requirements.

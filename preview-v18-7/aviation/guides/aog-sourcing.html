Aviation procurement guide

AOG Procurement Requests: Information That Reduces Delays

Preparation steps for urgent sourcing without promising stock or response times.

All buyer guides ↗

Make the urgency actionable

AOG requests can move quickly only when part identity, required condition and documentation are clear. State the actual requested delivery window and operational criticality without assuming any supplier has material on hand.

Send a precise P/N list

Use one row per part: manufacturer, P/N, quantity, required condition, optional alternate condition and known application. Serial or effectivity information should be included when it changes sourcing eligibility.

Separate stock from readiness

Stock availability, inspection completion, document readiness, export clearance and transport booking are different milestones. Request evidence and an estimated timeline for each relevant step.

Validate transport assumptions

Confirm pickup location, export/import responsibility, customs documentation and airline/ground transport constraints before communicating a firm ETA.

Review regulatory constraints early

Any controlled aerospace item requires appropriate screening and disclosure of legally required end-user, end-use and destination data. Urgency never overrides applicable law.

Record offer changes

Maintain one versioned comparison of supplier quotes so a revised lead time or condition is not confused with the initial offer.

Buying context

What to know before requesting a quote

Galley equipment includes inserts, carts and installed units whose fit and electrical interfaces may vary by aircraft and monument configuration.

For ovens, beverage makers and inserts, add connector/power details, installation position and any qualification requirements. For carts, include configuration and restraint compatibility.

Used galley hardware may have cosmetic and functional condition differences; specify inspection criteria before asking for a firm quote.

Product scope

Typical components for RFQ preparation

  • ovens and inserts
  • beverage makers
  • galley carts
  • latches and fittings
  • galley electrical parts

Information to include

insert P/N, aircraft/galley configuration, electrical rating, dimensions and condition.

Also include quantity, condition, delivery details and any required inspection, traceability or certificate package.

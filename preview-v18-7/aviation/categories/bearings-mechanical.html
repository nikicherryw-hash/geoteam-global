Buying context

What to know before requesting a quote

Aerospace bearing procurement should preserve the complete part identifier, including suffixes, tolerances, seals and material requirements. Generic industrial bearings are not automatically aviation-approved equivalents.

Share the drawing number or approved specification when available and identify required packaging, preservative condition and manufacturer traceability.

Any cross-reference remains a sourcing lead until application suitability and approved technical equivalence have been independently confirmed.

Product scope

Typical components for RFQ preparation

  • precision bearings
  • rod ends
  • spherical bearings
  • mechanical joints
  • specialty components

Information to include

full marking, dimensions, material, part suffix and aviation specification.

Also include quantity, condition, delivery details and any required inspection, traceability or certificate package.

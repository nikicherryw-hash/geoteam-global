Buying context

What to know before requesting a quote

Interior components require more than dimensional matching: color, trim, configuration and applicable material standards can affect acceptance. Provide both product references and cabin context.

Seating and interior requests should identify aircraft/operator configuration when permitted, the installation location and required supporting documents.

A sourcing discussion does not establish compliance with flammability, airworthiness or operator approval requirements.

Product scope

Typical components for RFQ preparation

  • seat components
  • cabin fittings
  • interior panels
  • lighting
  • stowage and trim

Information to include

aircraft and cabin configuration, P/N, color/finish, fire compliance and condition.

Also include quantity, condition, delivery details and any required inspection, traceability or certificate package.

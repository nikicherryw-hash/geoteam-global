Buying context

What to know before requesting a quote

Connectivity and inflight entertainment sourcing often depends on system generation and integration agreements, not just the name on a unit. Identify installed equipment and approved configuration.

Include the functional unit type, required software or firmware status, interface and licensing context when known. Some systems require specialist provisioning.

This catalog is an RFQ routing reference; it does not claim access to proprietary software or rights to reconfigure managed systems.

Product scope

Typical components for RFQ preparation

  • seatback equipment
  • connectivity modules
  • cabin network units
  • IFE accessories
  • electrical interface components

Information to include

system generation, P/N, aircraft configuration, software status and interfaces.

Also include quantity, condition, delivery details and any required inspection, traceability or certificate package.

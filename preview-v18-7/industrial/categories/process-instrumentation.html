Engineering context

Why technical identification matters

An instrument’s sensing technology must be matched to medium, pressure, temperature, turbulence and installation constraints.

For flow measurement, record line size and process conditions; for level, record vessel geometry and the product properties.

Do not present a catalog cross-reference as a validated measurement application or Ex approval.

Questions

Technical procurement FAQ

What information is needed to request an offer?

Start with process medium, range, output protocol, wetted materials, process connection and calibration requirement. Also specify quantity, condition, destination and acceptable documentation.

Can a similar model be used?

A suggested replacement must be evaluated against original design requirements, applicable standards and the machine or process owner’s approval. A listing or part number similarity alone is not evidence of equivalence.

Is this an inventory or authorized dealer catalog?

No. Brand names are identification references; availability and commercial terms are confirmed individually after a specific RFQ.

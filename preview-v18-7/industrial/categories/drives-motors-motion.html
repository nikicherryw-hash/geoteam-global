Engineering context

Why technical identification matters

Motor control can fail at installation despite a close model match if the voltage class, fieldbus option or control-board revision differs.

The RFQ should separate motor data, drive data, braking accessories and any required filter or enclosure class.

Never assume an equivalent drive preserves functional safety, fieldbus behavior or motor thermal protection.

Equipment scope

Typical components and systems

  • VFDs and inverters
  • soft starters
  • servo drives
  • industrial electric motors
  • encoders and braking units

Required RFQ data

drive and motor exact model, rated power, supply voltage, control mode, encoder interface and duty cycle.

Additionally include full model code, quantity, required documentation, delivery location and expected date. Technical approval is separate from sourcing.

Questions

Technical procurement FAQ

What information is needed to request an offer?

Start with drive and motor exact model, rated power, supply voltage, control mode, encoder interface and duty cycle. Also specify quantity, condition, destination and acceptable documentation.

Can a similar model be used?

A suggested replacement must be evaluated against original design requirements, applicable standards and the machine or process owner’s approval. A listing or part number similarity alone is not evidence of equivalence.

Is this an inventory or authorized dealer catalog?

No. Brand names are identification references; availability and commercial terms are confirmed individually after a specific RFQ.

Engineering context

Why technical identification matters

A matching nominal diameter does not establish suitable controllability, pressure drop, seat leakage class or actuator torque.

Specify fluid state, temperature, minimum/normal/maximum flow and upstream/downstream pressures, plus any shutdown duty.

Final valve selection and safety instrumented function changes require engineering and compliance approval.

Equipment scope

Typical components and systems

  • globe and rotary control valves
  • valve positioners
  • pneumatic actuators
  • electric actuators
  • solenoid pilots and accessories

Required RFQ data

valve type, DN/NPS, PN/ASME class, Cv/Kv, trim, fail action, actuator and signal interface.

Additionally include full model code, quantity, required documentation, delivery location and expected date. Technical approval is separate from sourcing.

Questions

Technical procurement FAQ

What information is needed to request an offer?

Start with valve type, DN/NPS, PN/ASME class, Cv/Kv, trim, fail action, actuator and signal interface. Also specify quantity, condition, destination and acceptable documentation.

Can a similar model be used?

A suggested replacement must be evaluated against original design requirements, applicable standards and the machine or process owner’s approval. A listing or part number similarity alone is not evidence of equivalence.

Is this an inventory or authorized dealer catalog?

No. Brand names are identification references; availability and commercial terms are confirmed individually after a specific RFQ.

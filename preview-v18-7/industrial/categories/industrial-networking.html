Engineering context

Why technical identification matters

A device that supports Ethernet may not support the required industrial protocol, redundancy or deterministic timing features.

Provide switch and gateway part numbers together with topology details, cybersecurity policy and environmental conditions.

Remote access products require security review and owner authorization; the RFQ is not a request to configure remote access.

Equipment scope

Typical components and systems

  • industrial Ethernet switches
  • fieldbus gateways
  • remote I/O networking
  • fiber media converters
  • industrial connectors

Required RFQ data

network protocol, switch port count, fiber/copper media, management features, voltage and firmware.

Additionally include full model code, quantity, required documentation, delivery location and expected date. Technical approval is separate from sourcing.

Questions

Technical procurement FAQ

What information is needed to request an offer?

Start with network protocol, switch port count, fiber/copper media, management features, voltage and firmware. Also specify quantity, condition, destination and acceptable documentation.

Can a similar model be used?

A suggested replacement must be evaluated against original design requirements, applicable standards and the machine or process owner’s approval. A listing or part number similarity alone is not evidence of equivalence.

Is this an inventory or authorized dealer catalog?

No. Brand names are identification references; availability and commercial terms are confirmed individually after a specific RFQ.

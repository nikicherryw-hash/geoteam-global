Engineering context

Why technical identification matters

Hydraulic valve variants can differ in spool function, solenoid voltage and mounting interfaces despite near-identical model strings.

For pumps, capture displacement, shaft, mounting flange and direction of rotation; for accumulators, specify vessel approval and service status.

Accumulators are pressure equipment. Substitutes cannot be accepted based on the nominal pressure alone.

Equipment scope

Typical components and systems

  • hydraulic power units
  • proportional and directional valves
  • pumps and motors
  • accumulators
  • hydraulic filtration

Required RFQ data

complete type code, pressure, displacement or rated flow, port pattern, seals and fluid compatibility.

Questions

Technical procurement FAQ

What information is needed to request an offer?

Start with complete type code, pressure, displacement or rated flow, port pattern, seals and fluid compatibility. Also specify quantity, condition, destination and acceptable documentation.

Can a similar model be used?

A suggested replacement must be evaluated against original design requirements, applicable standards and the machine or process owner’s approval. A listing or part number similarity alone is not evidence of equivalence.

Is this an inventory or authorized dealer catalog?

No. Brand names are identification references; availability and commercial terms are confirmed individually after a specific RFQ.

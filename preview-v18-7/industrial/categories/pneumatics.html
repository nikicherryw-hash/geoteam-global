Engineering context

Why technical identification matters

A cylinder may fit mechanically but have different cushioning, sensor grooves or rod-end connections. Compressed-air preparation affects repeatability and life.

Include photographs and drawings of ports, mounting and position sensors where available.

Replacement assemblies require confirmation of machine safety and pneumatic performance.

Equipment scope

Typical components and systems

  • pneumatic actuators and cylinders
  • solenoid valves
  • FRL air treatment
  • vacuum products
  • fittings and tubing

Required RFQ data

bore/stroke, mounting, valve function, actuation voltage, ports, seals and air treatment class.

Additionally include full model code, quantity, required documentation, delivery location and expected date. Technical approval is separate from sourcing.

Questions

Technical procurement FAQ

What information is needed to request an offer?

Start with bore/stroke, mounting, valve function, actuation voltage, ports, seals and air treatment class. Also specify quantity, condition, destination and acceptable documentation.

Can a similar model be used?

A suggested replacement must be evaluated against original design requirements, applicable standards and the machine or process owner’s approval. A listing or part number similarity alone is not evidence of equivalence.

Is this an inventory or authorized dealer catalog?

No. Brand names are identification references; availability and commercial terms are confirmed individually after a specific RFQ.

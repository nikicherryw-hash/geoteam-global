Engineering context

Why technical identification matters

Pump model names alone are insufficient to evaluate hydraulics, materials, NPSH margins or seal reliability.

Supply both required and current operating curves if possible, plus suction conditions and continuous/intermittent duty.

An alternative pump must be engineered against the complete system curve, materials and installation envelope.

Equipment scope

Typical components and systems

  • centrifugal pumps
  • multistage pumps
  • dosing pumps
  • progressive cavity pumps
  • pump spares and service kits

Required RFQ data

duty point (flow and head), medium, solids, viscosity, temperature, motor speed and seal specification.

Additionally include full model code, quantity, required documentation, delivery location and expected date. Technical approval is separate from sourcing.

Questions

Technical procurement FAQ

What information is needed to request an offer?

Start with duty point (flow and head), medium, solids, viscosity, temperature, motor speed and seal specification. Also specify quantity, condition, destination and acceptable documentation.

Can a similar model be used?

A suggested replacement must be evaluated against original design requirements, applicable standards and the machine or process owner’s approval. A listing or part number similarity alone is not evidence of equivalence.

Is this an inventory or authorized dealer catalog?

No. Brand names are identification references; availability and commercial terms are confirmed individually after a specific RFQ.

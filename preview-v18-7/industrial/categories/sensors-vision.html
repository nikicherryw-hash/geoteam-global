Engineering context

Why technical identification matters

Industrial sensors with the same outer dimensions may differ in PNP/NPN wiring, normally-open logic, sensing distance and commissioning requirements.

Quote the complete part number and supply a wiring diagram or connector image when legible.

Selection of an alternative requires verification of machine interfaces and hazardous-area approvals if applicable.

Equipment scope

Typical components and systems

  • inductive and capacitive sensors
  • photoelectric sensors
  • rotary encoders
  • vision cameras and lighting
  • safety light curtains

Required RFQ data

sensing principle, measuring range, output logic, connector, housing, IP rating and response time.

Additionally include full model code, quantity, required documentation, delivery location and expected date. Technical approval is separate from sourcing.

Questions

Technical procurement FAQ

What information is needed to request an offer?

Start with sensing principle, measuring range, output logic, connector, housing, IP rating and response time. Also specify quantity, condition, destination and acceptable documentation.

Can a similar model be used?

A suggested replacement must be evaluated against original design requirements, applicable standards and the machine or process owner’s approval. A listing or part number similarity alone is not evidence of equivalence.

Is this an inventory or authorized dealer catalog?

No. Brand names are identification references; availability and commercial terms are confirmed individually after a specific RFQ.

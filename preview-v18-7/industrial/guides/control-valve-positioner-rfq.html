Control Valve and Positioner RFQ: Key Design Data

Create a technically meaningful inquiry for valves, actuators and positioners.

All guides ↗

Capture fluid and process conditions

Include service medium, phase, design temperature, minimum/normal/maximum flow and inlet/outlet pressures. Valve sizing and noise/cavitation assessments depend on these values.

Name the valve construction

Identify body size, pressure rating, trim/material, end connections, seat leakage class and fail position. A DN label is not equivalent to a full specification.

Match actuation

State available instrument air pressure or power supply, actuator type, required stroke/torque and open/close timing.

Positioner details

Record control signal, HART/fieldbus requirement, diagnostics, area classification and mechanical mounting kit.

Approval and documentation

Require a supplier deviation list, proposed data sheet and any necessary test/certification evidence. Safety-instrumented applications need independent engineering approval.

Industrial Procurement Documentation and Incoterms

Practical checkpoints for comparing international industrial supply offers.

All guides ↗

Scope the exact goods

Confirm models, quantities, test requirements, HS classification where relevant and packing method before comparing freight cost.

Name the place

Always state an Incoterms rule and a precisely named point, alongside the version when relevant. A bare “DAP” without destination is ambiguous.

Separate origin and dispatch

Country of manufacture, seller’s location, dispatch origin and transit route are different attributes and must not be conflated.

Document checklist

Depending on the goods and contract, request invoice, packing list, certificates of conformity, origin documents, test/calibration reports and export documents.

Compliance preflight

Validate destination, end user, application, sanctions/export-control constraints and payment/shipment feasibility. Do not invent or conceal required declarations.

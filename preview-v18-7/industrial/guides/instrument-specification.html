Specify Pressure, Flow and Level Instruments Correctly

Essential application parameters for process measurement inquiries.

All guides ↗

Define the measurement duty

State the fluid or gas, measurement range, normal operating point, temperature and pressure. Mention viscosity, solids and conductivity when these affect sensor technology.

Mechanical and wetted design

Specify process connection, diaphragm/seal, wetted materials, pipe size or vessel geometry. Check the material against chemical compatibility rather than extrapolating from generic stainless steel.

Signal and control

Record 4–20 mA, HART, IO-Link, fieldbus or digital protocol, supply voltage and any hazardous-area requirements. Required device setup and parameterization should be identified.

Calibration and verification

State whether a calibration certificate, stated uncertainty or traceable test is required. Clarify whether commissioning or site calibration is in scope.

Compare on performance

Assess accuracy, repeatability, turndown, response, diagnostic capabilities and environmental rating, not just purchase price.

VFD and Motor RFQ: Ratings, Interfaces and Duty

How to avoid drive mismatches involving power, supply, control and installation.

All guides ↗

Motor nameplate details

Collect rated voltage, current, kW, frequency, rpm, service factor and efficiency class. Provide mechanical size and shaft details for motor replacement.

Drive electrical details

Specify supply phase/voltage, motor output current, overload rating, switching strategy and optional brake resistors or filters. A headline kW figure is not enough.

Controls and automation

Identify analog/digital I/O, communication option cards, STO and other safety requirements. Record encoder or resolver feedback when used.

Installation envelope

Confirm IP or NEMA enclosure, cooling, ambient temperature, mounting clearance, EMC conditions and altitude if material.

Commissioning responsibility

Parameter conversion, functional testing and machinery safety assessments should be performed by competent personnel using manufacturer documentation.

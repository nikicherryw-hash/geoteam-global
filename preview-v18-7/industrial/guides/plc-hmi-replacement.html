PLC, HMI and I/O Replacement: Compatibility Checklist

What to verify before ordering automation controllers, operator panels or distributed I/O.

All guides ↗

Identify the assembly, not just the CPU

Capture CPU, rack/backplane, power supply, communication modules, fieldbus options and I/O cards. Many PLC families have restrictions across hardware revisions and firmware generations.

Record the software environment

Ask the machine owner for the controller project version, licensing requirements and firmware constraints when those details can be lawfully shared. An HMI with a matching size may need a different runtime.

Verify interfaces

List I/O voltage/current, terminal style, network protocol, connector type and environmental ratings. When replacing safety hardware, identify the safety function without disclosing credentials or proprietary code.

Check obsolete variants

When a part is discontinued, seek manufacturer guidance, documented migration paths and a qualified integrator review. A supplier’s cross-reference is a sourcing clue, not proof of compatibility.

Acceptance evidence

Before shipment, require nameplate/order-code photos and documentation matched to the ordered revision. Commissioning and safety validation remain the buyer’s responsibility.

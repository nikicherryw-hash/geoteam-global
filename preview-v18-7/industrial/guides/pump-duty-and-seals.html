Pump Duty Point, NPSH and Mechanical Seals

The minimum system data needed to source a pump or evaluate an alternative.

All guides ↗

Measure the required duty

State flow in m³/h and total dynamic head in metres, plus design and typical operating points. Where possible, send the system curve and existing pump curve.

Describe the liquid

Include density, viscosity, temperature, solids concentration and corrosiveness. For abrasive or hazardous service, state the seal flushing and containment requirements.

Suction-side limitations

Record suction pressure, liquid level, site elevation and available NPSH. An alternative must be checked against the supplier’s NPSHr at relevant flow rates.

Mechanical and electric constraints

Specify nominal speed, available kW and supply, suction/discharge sizes, impeller or casing materials, connection pattern and available installation envelope.

Technical evaluation

Compare efficiency, permitted operating region, material and seal compatibility, vibration limits and maintenance access. Require engineering sign-off for replacements.

Test Reports, Calibration and Incoming Inspection

Avoid confusion between factory tests, calibration and conformity declarations.

All guides ↗

Clarify the evidence type

A certificate of conformity is not the same as a calibration certificate. A functional test may not report the uncertainty required for metrology.

Define acceptance criteria

For measured instruments identify tolerances, reference standards, measurement points and required reporting units.

Check scope and dates

Review calibration service scope, equipment serial, calibration date and any requested traceability.

Incoming verification

Compare physical labels and received quantities to the quote and packing list, then inspect packaging and any preservation requirements.

Escalate deviations

Quarantine mismatched goods pending technical or contractual decision. Procurement personnel should not quietly accept technical substitutions.

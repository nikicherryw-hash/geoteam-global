Obsolete Industrial Parts and Alternative Proposals

A disciplined process for discontinued PLC, drive, bearing and process components.

All guides ↗

Verify discontinuation

Use a manufacturer or authoritative channel statement where possible. A reseller declaring “obsolete” is not definitive evidence of manufacturer lifecycle status.

Preserve the installed requirement

Capture the full P/N, firmware, application constraints, photos and related drawings. Do not delete suffixes to force a match in a catalog.

Classify sourcing paths

Separate original old stock, used/refurbished inventory, manufacturer migration alternatives and engineered substitutes. Record condition and warranty independently.

Assess substitution risk

Identify electrical, dimensional, software, process safety and compliance deviations and send them for qualified review before recommending purchase.

Document the decision

Record who approved the technical equivalent, test scope and acceptance criteria. A marketplace listing does not by itself establish authenticity or compatibility.

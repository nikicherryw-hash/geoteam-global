WHAT WE SOURCE

Technical parts. Clear sourcing options.

GeoTeam Global works from the exact information a buyer has: a manufacturer reference, equipment model, drawing or multi-line BOM. We research possible sources and clarify the documentation, condition and delivery requirements before a commercial offer is confirmed.

Industrial Automation & Controls

PLC modules, HMI panels, drives, sensors, switchgear, industrial networking, hydraulics and pneumatic components. Exact part number and revision are important for compatibility.

Aviation Components & MRO

Aircraft-related components, avionics, accessories, ground-support and test equipment. Enquiries may require traceability, condition and applicable documentation checks.

Electronics & Semiconductors

Integrated circuits, electronic modules, connectors, power electronics and discontinued parts. The reference, manufacturer and supporting documentation matter.

Machinery Spares & Equipment

CNC and machine tool spares, pumps, motors, electromechanical assemblies, precision components and equipment-related parts.

Manufacturer references

Examples of manufacturers appearing in technical sourcing enquiries include Siemens, ABB, Schneider Electric, Phoenix Contact, Bosch Rexroth, Eaton, SMC, HERMLE and DMG MORI. Naming a manufacturer does not establish an authorised dealer relationship or current stock.

What happens after an enquiry?

Our team reviews product identification, possible channels, documentation, quantity, destination and relevant trade requirements. Any availability, price and delivery estimate must be confirmed with the proposed source.
